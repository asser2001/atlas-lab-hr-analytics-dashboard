## 📌 Executive Summary
* **Total Employees:** 1,470
* **Active Employees:** 1,233
* **Inactive Employees:** 237
* **Attrition Rate:** 16%

* **Avg Job Satisfaction:** 3.43

## 📊 Dashboard Modules & Key Insights

### 1. Overview Page
* **Gender Breakdown:**
  * Female: 675 (46%)
  * Male: 651 (44%)
  * Non-Binary: 124 (8%)
  * Prefer Not To Say: 20
* **Active Workforce Distribution by Department:**
  * **Technology:** 828 (Software Engineer: 247, Data Scientist: 199, Machine Learning Engineer: 136)
  * **Sales:** 354
  * **Human Resources:** 51
* **Top Roles by Headcount:** Sales Executive (327), Software Engineer (294), Data Scientist (261), Machine Learning Engineer (146), Senior Software Engineer (132).

---

### 2. Attrition Analysis Page
* **Overall Attrition Rate:** 16% (237 inactive employees)
* **Attrition by Gender:**
  * Male: 36%
  * Female: 32%
  * Non-Binary: 32%
* **Attrition by Marital Status & Business Travel:**
  * Single employees with *Some Travel* represent the highest volume of attrition.
* **Attrition by Department & Role:**
  * High attrition concentrated in **Technology** (Data Scientists: 62, Software Engineers: 47) and **Sales** (Sales Executives: 57).

---

### 3. Demographics Page
* **Age Range:**
  * **Oldest Employee:** 51 years old
  * **Youngest Employee:** 18 years old
* **Age Bin Concentration:**
  * The highest employee concentration falls in the **20** and **25** age bins, dominated by Female and Male employees.

---

### 4. Performance & Satisfaction Page
* **Average Performance Rating:** 3.47
* **Average Job Satisfaction:** 3.43
* **Performance Matrix:** Breakdown of manager ratings vs. employee self/peer evaluations across 1,470 staff members.
* **Job Satisfaction vs. Overtime:**
  * Employees working OverTime (*Yes*): 3.46 (50.4%)
  * Employees without OverTime (*No*): 3.41 (49.6%)

---

## 🔍 Filters Available
* **Year:** All
* **Department:** All (Technology, Sales, Human Resources)
* **Gender:** All (Female, Male, Non-Binary, Prefer Not To Say)

---

## 🚀 How to Open
1. Download the `.pbix` file from this repository.
2. Open using **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the navigation panel on the left to switch between **Overview**, **Attrition**, **Demographics**, and **Performance** pages.
